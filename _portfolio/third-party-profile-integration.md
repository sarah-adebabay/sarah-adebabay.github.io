I delivered the backend for LinkedIn Profile's first third-party integration, with Duolingo, and worked on expanding integrations to more partners.

Because the integration brought new partner data onto member profiles, I also built a data takeout service for that third-party data, handled PII and sensitive data, and kept GDPR and data compliance standards current.
