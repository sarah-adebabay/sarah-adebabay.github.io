With UC Berkeley's Design for Equity (D4E) Lab, I studied Adaptive Equity-Oriented Pedagogy (AEP) in computer science classrooms.

- Researched AEP’s effectiveness in reducing stereotype threat and boosting academic achievement in undergraduate STEM courses; advised EECS faculty on applying AEP
- Recorded qualitative field notes in classes and coded them for student-instructor interactions and student engagement
- Conducted statistical analyses in Stata comparing AEP and control classroom test scores
- Translated mixed-methods findings, including student surveys, into weekly feedback to adapt instruction
