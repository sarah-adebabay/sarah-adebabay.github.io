With UC Berkeley's Design for Equity (D4E) Lab, I studied Adaptive Equity-Oriented Pedagogy (AEP) in computer science classrooms.

- Advised CS professors on applying AEP in their courses
- Sat in on classes and took qualitative field notes on student-teacher interactions and engagement
- Coded field notes and student interviews
- Used Stata to test for statistically significant differences between AEP and control classrooms

Survey and interview findings were used to improve the course throughout the semester.
