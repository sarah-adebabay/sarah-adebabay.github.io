During Google's Software Product Sprint, my team built FUNdamentals, a web app that teaches early education concepts. Learners explore the alphabet, colors, and numbers, then practice with games and quizzes.

**What I built**

- The alphabet page, linked from the colors section
- Sounds that play each letter aloud when a learner clicks it

**Built with** HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, deployed on Google App Engine.
