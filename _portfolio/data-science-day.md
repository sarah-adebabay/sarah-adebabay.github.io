Data Science Day grew out of a question I kept coming back to: what happens when young people get the tools to test their own hypotheses about the world, and to ask whether the data behind a claim is fair?

**What we built.** An ethics-centered data science curriculum, run as an edition of the All About STEM outreach program through the Black Engineering and Science Student Association (BESSA) at UC Berkeley. Students worked with real data, formed and tested hypotheses, and reasoned about the ethical questions behind the numbers.

**How.** I co-designed the lessons with students as well as for them, using participatory design in lesson planning. The program was funded through grants and sponsorships I raised as NSBE External Vice President.

**What we learned.** I measured impact with pre and post surveys across 98 K-12 students and wrote a formal findings report. The project was my Education minor senior capstone, and it shaped my research focus on epistemic agency in STEM.

**Highlights**

- Launched and co-led a K-12 outreach event teaching 98 Black students data science and machine learning through a justice-centered curriculum, including Hidden Figures and Coded Bias screenings, guided discussions, and an undergraduate and industry panel
- Designed workshops, inspired by UCLA’s Introduction to Data Science (IDS) curriculum and Computer Science Teachers Association (CSTA) K-12 CS Standards and built on CODAP and Code.org
- Co-designed with two high school students prior to the event: an original activity using US Census data to visualize income inequalities faced by marginalized communities
- Surveyed students before and after the event: many did not know what STEM stood for beforehand, and higher percentages reported excitement about pursuing STEM careers and higher education; parents shared it was the first time their children were exposed to people who looked like them in STEM
- Raised over $36,000 in grants and sponsorships for Chromebooks, hotspots, and care packages, with over $10,000 left to fund two future events
- Recruited, trained, and managed 16 volunteers to facilitate workshops

Advisor: Professor Erin Murphy-Graham
