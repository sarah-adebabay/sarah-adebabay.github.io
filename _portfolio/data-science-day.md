Data Science Day grew out of a question I kept coming back to: what happens when young people get the tools to test their own hypotheses about the world, and to ask whether the data behind a claim is fair?

**What we built.** An ethics-centered data science curriculum, run as an edition of the All About STEM outreach program through the Black Engineering and Science Student Association (BESSA) at UC Berkeley. Students worked with real data, formed and tested hypotheses, and reasoned about the ethical questions behind the numbers.

**How.** I co-designed the lessons with students as well as for them, using participatory design in lesson planning. The program was funded through grants and sponsorships I raised as NSBE External Vice President.

**What we learned.** I measured impact with pre and post surveys across 98 K-12 students and wrote a formal findings report. The project was my Education minor senior capstone, and it shaped my research focus on epistemic agency in STEM.
