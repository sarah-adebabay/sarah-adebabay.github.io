Penny is an Android app that teaches financial literacy by helping young people, from middle school through college, save toward real goals: a phone, a car, or college. Kids set goals, deposit money toward them, and earn badges for good money habits. Parents can monitor their child's progress and approve transactions.

**What I built**

- The parent allowance system: adding, editing, and deleting a child's allowance, and showing it on the child's profile and the parent's detail view
- Automatic payment setup, with confirmation steps that feed into each goal's detail page
- Deposits toward goals and goal cancellation
- A cleaner layout for the goal detail page

**Built with** Java, Android, Parse, and Firebase Cloud Messaging.
