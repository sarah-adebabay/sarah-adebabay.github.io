As one of 30 undergraduate fellows in the UC Berkeley Computational Approaches to Human Learning Lab x Schmidt Futures Educational Data Science and Learning Engineering Fellowship, I worked with two teammates on why K-12 teachers and administrators hesitate to adopt STEM edtech.

- Pitched YedTech, a teacher review and recommendation platform for edtech products and companies, to Schmidt Futures, on a team of three
- Built market strategy and competitive analysis drawing on user interviews with K-12 teachers and administrators on STEM edtech hesitancy
