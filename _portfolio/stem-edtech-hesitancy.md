As one of 30 undergraduate fellows in the UC Berkeley x Schmidt Futures Educational Data Science and Learning Engineering Fellowship, I worked with two teammates on the question of why K-12 teachers and administrators hesitate to adopt STEM EdTech tools.

We proposed a teacher co-op model built on partnerships with EdTech companies, and pitched it to Schmidt Futures for funding in a 15-minute team presentation.
